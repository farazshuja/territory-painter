How to Play

  • Characters: 2 boxes of 20×20 px.

    • Player 2 (Light Purple) starts at Bottom-Left.
  • Controls: P1: WASD • P2: ▲▼◄►.
    Hold to paint continuously. Xbox: left stick or D-pad; Menu opens settings, View restarts.
  • Points: +10 points per new square painted!
  • Rules: You cannot cross or step onto the opponent's painted path unless powered up.
  • ✨ Arcade Pickups:
    • 🍟 🍔 🍩 Junk food: Slows you for 5, 10, or 15 seconds.
    • 🍎 🫐 🥗 Good food: Speeds you up for 5, 10, or 15 seconds.
    • 👟 🛹 Shoes / skateboard: Big speed boosts for 8 or 15 seconds.
    • 🍏 Golden Apple: Breach and steal territory for 15 seconds.
    • ❄ Snowflake: Freezes your opponent for 5 seconds.
